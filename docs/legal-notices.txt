Evidence and geographic interpretation
 USGS records describe reported seismic activity and can be revised. EONET provides curated references with potentially approximate spatial and temporal extents. GDACS presents indicative disaster assessments and modelled impacts. MET Norway supplies model forecasts for a selected point; forecasts are not observed disaster events.  Source, issue, occurrence, valid and capture times have different meanings. Displayed locations are source-reported points or explicitly approximate representatives, not verified impact boundaries. This preview does not estimate personal exposure, safe routes, casualties or safety. Source records may refer to the same event and are not a count of unique disasters.  Scythe offers short explanations tied to the displayed evidence. It is not a professional emergency assessment. For protective action, verify information with the responsible local authority. The directory does not dispatch assistance.
